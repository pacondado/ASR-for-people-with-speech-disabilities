# Whisper LoRA Fine-tuning

**Speech recognition that adapts to your voice.**

> 🚧 **Coming soon — available on 16 November 2026.**
> Watch ⭐ this repository to be notified when it is released.

## About

Speech recognition tools such as OpenAI's Whisper work well for typical speech, but often struggle with voices that are different: people with speech disorders, strong accents or specific vocabulary.

This project will make it easy for **anyone**, with or without technical knowledge, to adapt a Whisper model to their own voice using a set of their own recordings.

## Who is it for?

- People with speech disorders who want a speech recogniser that understands them.
- Speech therapists, caregivers and families.
- Researchers working with atypical or accented speech.

## What you will need

- Recordings of your voice and their transcriptions.
- A computer with an **NVIDIA GPU** or an **Apple Silicon Mac** (M1 or later).

## Privacy

Everything runs on your own computer. Your recordings never leave it.
